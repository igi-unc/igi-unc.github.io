Relationship to PIRL & specimen/clinical partners

IGI shares principal investigators and partial staff overlap with the Personalized Immunotherapy Research Lab (PIRL), which pursues a broader set of smaller-scale research projects while IGI focuses at larger scale on tumor-antigen datasets and clinical vaccine trials. Specimen procurement, sequencing, and clinical manufacturing draw on UNC’s Screening Live Cancer Explants (SLiCE) Core, the UNC Lineberger Tissue Procurement Facility, the UNC Pleural Fluid Registry, the Rare Cancer Research Foundation, the UNC Department of Genetics, and the UNC Advanced Cellular Therapeutics GMP Facility.
