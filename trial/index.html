Clinical Trial

Head-to-head vaccine platform comparison & open trial paperwork

Personalized cancer vaccine trials usually change target selection and vaccine delivery at the same time, making it hard to separate poor antigen predictions from weak vaccine immunogenicity. Our clinical program holds target selection constant across arms so vaccine platforms can be compared directly.

Design & immune readouts

  • Starting arms: synthetic long peptides + poly-ICLC (default arm) compared against Moderna-style mRNA-LNP, with additional vaccine platforms added as new arms over time.
  • Shared target selection: all arms in a comparison use the same antigen-selection process across classical neoantigens, structural-variant reading frames, fusions, and cancer-testis antigens, informed by our tumor-antigen validation data.
  • Primary outcome: direct ex vivo CD8+ T-cell responses measured uniformly across arms.
  • Secondary outcomes: longitudinal TCR clone tracking in peripheral blood, paired with single-cell VDJ/expression profiling and pMHC:TCR specificity mapping of expanded clones.

Open trial documents

Alongside clinical and immunogenicity results, we publish our clinical protocols, IND and CMC packages, SOPs, and regulatory correspondence openly on GitHub so other academic groups can launch and iterate on AI-designed investigator-initiated trials.
