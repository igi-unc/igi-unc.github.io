Clinical Trial

Head-to-head vaccine platform comparison & open trial paperwork

Published personalized cancer vaccine trials change target selection and vaccine delivery at the same time, conflating antigen-prediction errors with insufficient vaccine potency. Our clinical program holds the antigen-selection process constant across arms so vaccine platforms can be compared directly.

Arms & immune readouts

  • Starting comparison: synthetic long peptides + poly-ICLC (default arm) versus Moderna-style mRNA-LNP, with additional vaccine platforms added as new arms over time.
  • Primary comparison: direct ex vivo CD8+ T-cell responses measured uniformly across every arm.
  • Secondary readout: longitudinal TCR clone tracking in blood, paired with single-cell VDJ/expression profiling and pMHC:TCR specificity mapping of vaccine-expanded clones.

Antigen selection

Candidate vaccine targets draw from classical neoantigens, structural-variant reading frames, fusions, and cancer-testis antigens using a combination of LENS, OpenVax 2 (under active development primarily by PIRL), PIRL long-read tools such as Exacto and Nexus, and standard community workflows such as ImmunoNX.

Open regulatory playbook

All clinical trial documents (protocols, IND and CMC templates, SOPs, and regulatory correspondence) are published openly on GitHub so other academic groups can launch and iterate on AI-designed investigator-initiated trials.
