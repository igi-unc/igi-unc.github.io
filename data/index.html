Open Data

Tumor antigen presentation, TCR specificity, and public archives

Everything IGI generates is released publicly without restriction, with one exception: germline genetic information (DNA and germline-carrying sequencing reads from patient normal or tumor samples), which is deposited in controlled-access archives (dbGaP and SRA) to protect patient privacy.

Tumor & T-cell profiling

Most predicted neoantigens never appear at meaningful abundance on tumor cells. Working closely with UNC’s Screening Live Cancer Explants (SLiCE) core for viable surgical tumor tissue alongside clinical biobanks across a wide range of solid and hematologic cancers, each specimen cohort combines:

  • Long-read DNA & single-cell RNA/TCR sequencing: tumor/normal whole-genome sequencing and full-length single-cell transcriptomes + paired αβ TCRs spanning point mutations, indels, structural variants, fusions, splice isoforms, and cancer-testis antigens.
  • Mass-spectrometry immunopeptidomics: targeted heavy-isotope standards, inclusion-list DDA, and personalized-proteome DIA to measure which candidate peptide-MHC complexes are actually presented on tumor cells.
  • High-throughput pMHC:TCR screening: functional deorphanization of tumor-specific TCRs from tumor-infiltrating lymphocytes (TILs) and tumor-associated T cells.
