Initiative for Generative Immunotherapy

UNC Lineberger Comprehensive Cancer Center, Chapel Hill, NC

The Initiative for Generative Immunotherapy (IGI) is an independent research unit at UNC Chapel Hill, started in 2026 with support from the OpenAI Foundation alongside the Personalized Immunotherapy Research Lab (PIRL).

We focus on two things:

  1. Measuring what tumors actually present to T cells. Most predicted neoantigens never reach the tumor surface. Across a broad set of cancer types, working closely with UNC’s Screening Live Cancer Explants (SLiCE) core and clinical biobanks, we pair long-read genome and single-cell RNA/TCR sequencing with mass-spectrometry immunopeptidomics (covering point mutations, indels, structural variants, fusions, and cancer-testis antigens) and high-throughput pMHC:TCR screening to deorphanize tumor-specific TCRs from TILs and tumor-associated T cells.
  2. Comparing vaccine platforms head-to-head in the clinic. Past vaccine trials change target selection and vaccine delivery at the same time, making it hard to tell why a vaccine worked or failed. Our clinical trial holds the antigen-selection pipeline fixed across arms, starting with synthetic long peptides + poly-ICLC as the default arm against Moderna-style mRNA-LNP and adding new arms over time. Arms are compared on direct ex vivo CD8+ T-cell responses, with longitudinal TCR clone tracking as a secondary outcome.

As a smaller, longer-horizon effort, we also work on targeted lipid nanoparticles (tLNPs) to deliver discovered TCRs as in vivo TCR-Ts or, with dendritic-cell targeting, to deliver antigens as vaccines.

Open data & open trial documents

Everything we generate is public, except germline genetic information (DNA and germline-carrying reads from patient or tumor samples), which is deposited in controlled-access archives (dbGaP / SRA).

  • Presented pMHCs: mass-spectrometry immunopeptidomics spectra and validated targets (PRIDE, IEDB, GitHub)
  • TCRs & T-cell assays: deorphanized pMHC:TCR pairs, TIL and blood TCR repertoires, and direct ex vivo CD8+ readouts (VDJdb, AIRR, GitHub)
  • Somatic targets & expression: somatic variant calls, tumor-specific transcript/protein sequences, antigen predictions, and RNA expression matrices (GEO, GitHub)
  • Clinical trial paperwork: protocols, IND and CMC packages, SOPs, and regulatory filings (GitHub)

Code

Repositories are at github.com/igi-unc, alongside PIRL (LENS, RAFT, mhcgnomes) and OpenVax (MHCflurry, Vaxrank, Isovar, Varcode, Topiary):

  • igi-syn-seq: synthetic 15-assay tumor/normal truth sets (IGI-SYN-SEQ-01, IGI-SYN-SEQ-02) for benchmarking antigen callers
  • igi-lims: chain-of-custody LIMS tracking specimen and tube lineage from collection through sequencing and immunopeptidomics
  • igi-data-librarian: sequencing registry and automated RAFT / Nextflow QC
