Initiative for Generative Immunotherapy

UNC Lineberger Comprehensive Cancer Center

The Initiative for Generative Immunotherapy (IGI) is an independent research unit at UNC Chapel Hill, started in 2026 with support from the OpenAI Foundation. We release our datasets, clinical trial documents, and code publicly, focusing on two problems in personalized cancer vaccines:

  1. Measuring what tumors actually present to T cells. Most predicted tumor antigens never reach the cell surface. Across a wide range of cancers, we directly measure which candidate peptides are presented on tumor cells and which T-cell receptors recognize them, creating open training and benchmark data for antigen-selection models.
  2. Comparing vaccine platforms head-to-head in the clinic. Holding target selection constant across trial arms lets us directly compare how strongly different vaccine platforms elicit ex vivo CD8+ T-cell responses, while publishing all protocols, IND/CMC packages, and trial SOPs so other groups can build on them.
