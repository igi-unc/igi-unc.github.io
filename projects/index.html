Other Projects

Longer-horizon & supporting work outside the main data and trial effort

Alongside our two primary efforts (tumor antigen data generation and the multi-arm vaccine trial), we work on smaller supporting and exploratory projects:

  • Targeted lipid nanoparticles (tLNPs): developing antibody-targeted lipid nanoparticles to deliver discovered TCRs as in vivo TCR-Ts or, with dendritic-cell targeting, to deliver vaccine antigens.
  • Automated vaccine target review: building open-source agentic review tools to assist and speed up the manual locus-level QC, isoform inspection, and candidate adjudication step between pipeline output and vaccine manufacturing.

Broader, smaller-scale experimental and computational research projects across our shared investigators and lab members live at PIRL and on the PIRL Blog.
