Antigen selection, synthetic benchmarks, and lab infrastructure

Candidate antigen discovery

For preclinical tumor profiling and target validation, IGI sources candidate antigens from the union of four software stacks across PIRL, OpenVax, and the broader immunogenomics community:

  • Nexus & Exacto: PIRL tools for orchestrating Nextflow analyses and resolving expressed structural variants and mutant proteoforms from long-read DNA/RNA sequencing.
  • LENS: PIRL’s tumor antigen pipeline curating tumor-specific MHC ligands across somatic mutations, fusions, splice variants, endogenous retroviruses, viral proteins, and cancer-testis antigens, run reproducibly via RAFT.
  • OpenVax & OpenVax 2: open-source Python libraries and vaccine design tools (MHCflurry, Vaxrank, Isovar, Varcode, Topiary, mhctools, PyEnsembl) originally created for personalized vaccine trials at Mount Sinai and maintained at UNC, where PIRL leads development of OpenVax 2.
  • ImmunoNX: standard clinical immunogenomics and pVACtools workflow developed at Washington University.

IGI repositories (@igi-unc)

  • igi-syn-seq: generator and truth specifications for IGI-SYN-SEQ-01 and IGI-SYN-SEQ-02, synthetic 15-assay tumor/normal datasets (Illumina, PacBio HiFi/Kinnex, ONT, 10x GEX/TCR) for benchmarking antigen callers across every variant class.
  • igi-lims: chain-of-custody LIMS tracking specimen and tube lineage from tissue collection through cellular fractions, DNA/RNA extracts, peptide eluates, QC imports, and sequencing files.
  • igi-data-librarian: sequencing registry and automated RAFT / Nextflow quality-control runner.
